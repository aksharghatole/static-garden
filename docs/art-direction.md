# Botanical observatory art direction

Original procedural vector artwork; no external image assets copied into the game.

- Palette: night #0b1715, forest #1b3830, moss #83af78, sage #d5e5b5, amber #e9bb78.
- Aim for a calm greenhouse crossed with a remote satellite receiver.
- Plants have species-specific silhouettes; gold accents communicate a living signal.
- Stage transitions are brief and event-driven rather than continuous animation.
- Keep navigation, text labels and touch targets legible.

Inspiration research: [Pinterest Terra Futura](https://business.pinterest.com/en-ca/pinterest-predicts/2025/terra-futura/), [Pinterest 2026 Hobbies Trend Report](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/2026-pinterest-hobbies-trend-report/), [Pinterest palette](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/introducing-the-pinterest-palette/).
